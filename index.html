About Us

Welcome to Karuppan Kari Virunthu

Karuppan Kari Virunthu is a traditional South Indian non-vegetarian hotel located in Pannapalayam, Palladam .

We are known for serving authentic, flavourful regional dishes prepared with traditional South Indian flavours.

Come and enjoy delicious food with family and friends.
